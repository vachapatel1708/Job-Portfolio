Frequently Asked Questions

How can I create a job seeker account? +

Click on the Sign Up button and select Job Seeker. Complete your details and create your professional profile.

How can an HR post a job? +

HR users can create an account, complete their company profile and use the Post a Job section to publish job openings.

Is CareerConnect free for job seekers? +

Yes. Job seekers can create their profile, search for jobs and apply to suitable opportunities.
